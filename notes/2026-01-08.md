What an ordeal!

My whole issue with pfsense from the weeks before was already a huge rabbit hole, and now I've spent the last 5 days (about 10 hours total) trying to get my kubernetes cluster to serve a cloudflare tunnel pointing to my linkding app.

1 issue was that I had my tunnel-credentials in the wrong namespace.

Another issue, or several, was that my pfsense was causing extra work that I didn't predict, but should have.

Also, for some reason I had to include the cert.pem generated by a cloudflare tunnel when others didn't have to. Maybe things got updated for cloudflare that that is now required.

I also had to contend with using flux, kubectl, etc. so that when I changed a configmap or a secret, that the change could propagate safely throughout the cluster.

I've learned that namespaces are one of the first things you can look at for breaking changes. I also learned that.

I also learned that configmaps require a catch-all ingress rule for cloudflared deployments.

`flux reconcile` and `kubectl rollout restart` are very nice commands to know.
